## What they have deployed or contributed

The AI Diffusion Pathways initiative built and maintains the six-shift framework that structures this wiki, and has been the primary technical partner and knowledge transfer agent across the documented deployments. They built MahaVISTAAR (as technical partner to the Department of Agriculture, Maharashtra), supported the Ethiopia/ATI deployment as the first international transfer, and contributed to Amul/Sarlaben and Bihar Krishi. They curated 67 AI impact stories for the AI Commons, a process that surfaced the finding that more than half of builders could not articulate their own problem statement.

The initiative's primary contribution to the Cube is the six-orthogonal-shifts framework — the decomposition of AI deployment into six independent dimensions and 21 sub-components, with FROM→TO operational formulations for each. This framework is the capture lens for every pathway in this wiki.

The Blue Dots AI livelihoods pathway (Dharwad and Ghaziabad, 2024–25) was authored by Tushar Bansal, EkStep Foundation (May 2026). This source also confirms EkStep Foundation as the institution behind the AI Diffusion Pathways initiative — resolving the attribution uncertainty flagged in the prior ingest.

EkStep Foundation has extended the AI diffusion pathway to the livelihoods sector, applying the same six-dimension framework and DPG architecture to the problem of district-level local discovery failure. The Blue Dots AI platform is a new deployment class — not agriculture, not government advisory, but shared digital discovery infrastructure for district economies.

## When to connect with them

An adopter planning an AI deployment in government, agriculture, health, or education who wants to draw on the transferable assets (architecture specifications, governance frameworks, language pipeline methodology, model evaluation benchmarks, failure mode library) accumulated across prior deployments. Particularly valuable if: this is the first deployment of its kind in your institutional context, or if you are attempting a cross-country transfer of an existing pathway.
