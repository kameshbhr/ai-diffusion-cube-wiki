## What this wiki is

The AI Diffusion Cube is a knowledge base of AI deployment pathways — lived experience from people who have taken AI from idea to scale in government, agriculture, health, education, and other public-interest sectors. Its purpose is to dramatically compress the time, cost, and risk for the next person attempting a comparable deployment.

This is not a document library. Every source ingested is integrated — not filed. Pages are updated, contradictions flagged, cross-links strengthened. Knowledge compounds with every addition.

## Where the wiki is thin

- **No standalone pathway pages.** The current wiki has deployment and concept pages but no pathway pages structured for a specific next-adopter use case. These require direct provider contributions.
- **Dimension 3 (Institution):** C2 (resistance and non-transferability) is named and typed but the specific mechanisms are not documented. Providers are reluctant to discuss internal resistance.
- **Dimension 6 (Operating model):** F4 (pilot to deployment) is the most important and most under-documented sub-component. No current source describes the specific transition process for any named deployment.
- **F2 (Governance):** Procurement as failure mode is named; no solution pathway is documented.
- **Non-agriculture sectors:** All five documented deployments are agriculture. No health, education, or government service delivery pathways exist yet.
- **Workforce training at depth:** E2 evidence comes almost entirely from Indian government rotation contexts. Other institutional environments not documented.
