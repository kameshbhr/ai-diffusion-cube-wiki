# Livelihoods — Pathway Index

AI deployments in livelihoods typically focus on expanding access to information, services, or markets for people whose primary constraint is not awareness of what is available but the practical difficulty of navigating complex systems (scheme applications, market prices, veterinary advice, financial products) without intermediary support. In this sector, the AI advisory layer often plays the same role that a knowledgeable local agent would play — answering questions, routing people to the right service, and reducing the information asymmetry that keeps income at a lower level than it could be. The user population is often rural, often women, often low-literacy, and almost always voice-first by necessity rather than preference. Deployments that do not accommodate this constraint profile do not reach scale.

## Pathways in this sector

### [Amul Sarlaben](../pathways/amul-sarlaben.md)
**Geography:** India — Gujarat, 18,500+ villages | **Actor type:** Cooperative | **Status:** Active

AI veterinary and dairy advisory for 3.6 million milk producers, the majority women. Addresses veterinary access scarcity (1,400 vets for 3.6M farmers and 22M cattle) through 24/7 voice and app advisory grounded in 2 billion cooperative transactions and 30 million cattle records. Primary reference for cooperative-sector AI deployment, voice-first design for women users, and inherent sustainability through cooperative financial alignment.
