# Agriculture — Pathway Index

AI deployments in agriculture typically involve bridging a structural advisory gap: there are too few extension officers, veterinarians, or agronomists to serve the farmer population with timely, personalised advice. AI provides the scalable advisory layer; the challenge is ensuring that advice is accurate (grounded in local crop, soil, and weather data), trusted (by farmers who have reason to be sceptical of advice that doesn't account for their specific situation), and absorbed (by field workers who might otherwise see AI as a threat to their role). Deployments in this sector are also heavily infrastructure-dependent — they need weather data, market price feeds, scheme databases, and scientific knowledge from national institutions, and the ease or difficulty of accessing those data sources is often the primary deployment bottleneck.

## Pathways in this sector

### [MahaVistaar](../pathways/mahavistaar.md)
**Geography:** India — Maharashtra state | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active

The most thoroughly documented deployment in this wiki. Primary reference for technology architecture (federated data, self-hosted inference, moderation layer), ecosystem assembly (54 organisations), and cost management at scale. 342,000+ unique users, 440,000 queries/month, 97%+ satisfaction.

### [Bharat-VISTAAR](../pathways/bharat-vistaar.md)
**Geography:** India — national | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active

National-scale federation targeting 120 million farmers. Hub-and-spoke architecture connecting state and sector deployments. Rs 150 crore allocation. Primary reference for national-level coordination challenges and hub-and-spoke federation design.

### [Bihar Krishi](../pathways/bihar-krishi.md)
**Geography:** India — Bihar state, 38 districts | **Actor type:** Civil society | **Status:** Active

Civil society-led deployment with government partnership. 15,000+ extension workers trained before launch. 850,000+ registered farmers. Primary reference for pre-launch workforce training model and civil society-government partnership structure.

### [Amul Sarlaben](../pathways/amul-sarlaben.md)
**Geography:** India — Gujarat, 18,500+ villages | **Actor type:** Cooperative | **Status:** Active

Veterinary and dairy advisory for 3.6 million milk producers (majority women). Voice short code and app. Primary reference for cooperative-sector AI deployment, inherent sustainability alignment, and user trust through cooperative ownership.

### [Ethiopia ATI](../pathways/ethiopia-ati.md)
**Geography:** Ethiopia — national | **Actor type:** Government | **Status:** Active

National AI agricultural advisory system launched February 2026. 3-month commitment-to-launch. Fayda digital ID integration. Primary reference for deployment compression through architecture reuse and Africa-context DPI foundation.
