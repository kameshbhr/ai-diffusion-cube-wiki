# Bihar Krishi

**Organisation:** Government of Bihar (state)
**Sector:** Agriculture
**Geography:** Bihar state, India (all 38 districts)
**Scale:** 850,000+ farmers registered 🟡; 15,000+ extension workers trained across 38 districts 🟡; 38,000+ scheme applications submitted 🟡; 20–25% monthly engagement rate 🟡; 20 million farmers reached through digital outreach 🟡
**Period:** Active since May 2025 🟡; built under Bihar's 4th Agriculture Roadmap
**Contact / network:** [[people-orgs/ai-diffusion-pathways]]

## One-paragraph summary

Bihar Krishi is a state government agricultural AI deployment built independently — under Bihar's 4th Agriculture Roadmap, with MicroSave Consulting and Gates Foundation support — with different partners and under a different institutional logic from MahaVISTAAR. It is the primary source of evidence on the E2 training depth sub-component: it trained 15,000+ extension workers across 38 districts, and is the reference case for the challenge of building institutional knowledge deep enough to survive staff rotation. Since May 2025 it has reached 850,000+ registered farmers, handled 38,000+ scheme applications, and achieved 20–25% monthly engagement. When Bharat-VISTAAR launched, Bihar Krishi became its Bihar node — connecting to national knowledge base, AgriStack, and ICAR data; OAN amplified what Bihar had built, it did not originate it. Winner of the ET DigiTech Award 2025 (Gold) and SKOCH Award 2025 (Gold). 🟡

## Key facts for adopters

- **15,000+ extension workers trained across 38 districts.** 🟡 The largest documented workforce training effort across these deployments. Structured, deliberate, and scaled — different design problem from training end users.
- **Built independently with different partners.** 🟡 MicroSave Consulting and Gates Foundation support; under Bihar's 4th Agriculture Roadmap. Different from MahaVISTAAR's ecosystem and institutional logic.
- **850,000+ farmers registered, 38,000+ scheme applications since May 2025.** 🟡 At 20–25% monthly engagement rate, this represents sustained rather than one-off usage.
- **20 million farmers reached through digital outreach.** 🟡 Scale of awareness/reach vs. registered users distinction — 850,000 registered vs. 20 million reached.
- **In Indian government, extension officers rotate routinely.** 🟡 This makes E2 (training depth) a critical question: if knowledge stops at the first layer, it will not survive the second posting cycle. Bihar Krishi's scale of training is partly a response to this structural reality.
- **Test for adequate training depth:** Can a district official explain to a sceptical colleague why the AI recommended what it did? Can the system function when every individual who helped build it has moved to a different role? 🟡
- **Historically 1 field officer per 2,000 farmers in Bihar.** 🟡 Context for the structural gap: advisory capability that scales with population requires a different architecture than one that scales only with staff.
- **25% of Bihar's farmer population are women.** 🟡
- **Awards: ET DigiTech 2025 Gold, SKOCH 2025 Gold.** 🟡 National recognition prior to connection to Bharat-VISTAAR.
- **Connected to Bharat-VISTAAR as Bihar node at launch.** 🟡 OAN amplified what Bihar had built; it did not originate it. Bihar Krishi was "a state that built first on its own terms, then connected."
