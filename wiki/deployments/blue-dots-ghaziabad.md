**Organisation:** District Administration, Ghaziabad; Government of Uttar Pradesh
**Sector:** Livelihoods / District economy

**Scale:** 10,000+ local job openings visible from <10% SMB coverage in under 60 days; 4 months to meaningful activation

## One-paragraph summary

Ghaziabad is the second district deployment of the Blue Dots AI platform and the first replication site. Where Dharwad took 10 months to reach a self-sustaining ecosystem, Ghaziabad reached meaningful activation in 4 months — demonstrating that the pathway compresses with learning, DPGs, and a documented playbook. The headline evidence: when fewer than 10% of Ghaziabad's SMBs were surfaced on the shared digital map, over 10,000 local job openings became visible in under 60 days. The jobs had always been there. They were digitally dark. The anchor was the Chief Development Officer (CDO), who convened the first aggregators, held the rhythm throughout activation, and was the named individual who said "this runs in my name."

## Key facts for adopters

- **The discovery moment:** Over 10,000 local job openings became visible in under 60 days when fewer than 10% of Ghaziabad's SMBs were onboarded. National platforms showed fewer than 100 local listings for the same district at the same time. The jobs had always been there — they were digitally dark. 🟡
- **Compression from Dharwad:** 4 months to meaningful activation vs. 10 months in Dharwad. The reduction came from inheriting DPGs, a documented playbook, and Dharwad's failure modes — not from technology change. The technology was the same. 🟡
- **The named anchor:** The Chief Development Officer was the specific individual who convened the first aggregators and held the rhythm. The source is explicit: "the use case that activates fastest is not the most ambitious — it is the one where a named individual says 'this runs in my name.'" 🟡
- **The paradox made concrete:** A commerce graduate (named Kavita in the source document) was being urged to move to Delhi, convinced a career could not be built at home, while a manufacturing firm 2 km from her house was looking for someone exactly like her. An ITI graduate spent weeks searching while three SMBs within walking distance needed the same skillset. A woman returning to work after raising children found no nearby options, despite local employers who would hire her today if they knew she existed. 🟡
- **Placement conversion shift:** Below 10% at job fairs → above 50% in targeted drives, same district and same population. 🟡
- **Cost shift:** ₹500+ per interaction via field surveys and job fairs → ₹10 per interaction via voice AI. 🟡
- **State scaling:** Uttar Pradesh is scaling to multiple districts (2025–26) using Ghaziabad as the activation model. 🟡
