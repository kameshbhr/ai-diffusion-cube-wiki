# Blue Dots AI — Dharwad (Pioneer District)

**Organisation:** District Administration, Dharwad; Government of Karnataka
**Sector:** Livelihoods / District economy
**Geography:** Dharwad district, Karnataka, India
**Scale:** 300+ employers onboarded by one MSME association in 2 weeks; 500+ seekers onboarded by one ITI; self-sustaining ecosystem reached
**Period:** 2024 (10 months from decision to self-sustaining ecosystem)
**Contact / network:** [AI Diffusion Pathways / EkStep Foundation](../people-orgs/ai-diffusion-pathways.md)

## One-paragraph summary

Dharwad is the pioneer district deployment of the Blue Dots AI platform — India's first proof that shared digital discovery infrastructure can make a digitally dark district economy visible to itself. Before Blue Dots AI, jobs, workers, services, and schemes existed in proximity but could not find each other because the long tail of local economic activity was invisible on any digital map. In 10 months, Dharwad built a self-sustaining ecosystem: one MSME association onboarded 300+ employers in two weeks; one ITI onboarded 500+ seekers; private innovators began operating on their own economics once density was sufficient. Dharwad is the reference implementation from which Ghaziabad and subsequent districts learn and compress.

## Key facts for adopters

- **Pioneer timeline:** 10 months from decision to self-sustaining ecosystem, built without a prior model to follow. Subsequent districts should compress this substantially. 🟡
- **The density threshold:** The self-sustaining tipping point was reached when private actors — staffing firms, assessment providers, skilling organisations — began joining on their own economics, without continued government subsidy. In Dharwad, this threshold was crossed within the first three months of aggregator mobilisation. 🟡
- **Aggregator-led density:** One MSME association (employer/provider side) onboarded 300+ employers in two weeks. One ITI (seeker side) onboarded 500+ job-seekers. Mass onboarding via existing institutional relationships — not person-by-person field mobilisation. 🟡
- **The state sponsor anchor:** A state-level champion who provided political cover was the institutional spine that made the ecosystem real to other actors. Without this anchor, the ecosystem had no reason to hold together. 🟡
- **Voice as zero-barrier entry:** Any phone, any language, 2–3 minutes to become discoverable. No smartphone, literacy, or English required. 🟡
- **Consent-based data by architecture:** Data created by participants via voice interaction; participants own their Blue Dots; the state operator governs but does not own participant data. DPDP 2023 compliance built into the rails, not layered on after deployment. 🟡
