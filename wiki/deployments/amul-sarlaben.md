## One-paragraph summary

Amul's cooperative deployment is the fastest in the compression sequence — 3 weeks from pathway adoption to deployment — enabled by Amul's extraordinary existing data infrastructure (2 billion milk procurement transactions, 30 million cattle records, 50 years of cooperative history). The AI system, named "Sarlaben," reaches 3.6 million milk producers across 18,500+ villages in Gujarat via voice call in Gujarati, including feature phone and landline users. It enables younger family members who lack thirty years of dairy experience to access expert knowledge specific to their own animals. The deployment demonstrates the plus-one scaling pattern: Sarlaben advisory → AI-based booking for artificial insemination → microcredit via cooperative bank → Bharat Taxi via open network protocols. It also produced the clearest field evidence on the agency test: the positive outcome (capability without dependency) is documented; the risk (information replacing skill) is also named. Referenced by Prime Minister Modi at the India AI Impact Summit 2026. 🟡

## Key facts for adopters

- **3 weeks to deploy.** 🟡 Fastest in the compression sequence, enabled by deep existing data infrastructure (2 billion procurement transactions, 50 years cooperative data, 1,200+ veterinary doctor records). If you have comparable data depth, this is the benchmark.
- **Fine-tuned smaller open-source models outperformed larger commercial APIs.** 🟡 94% accuracy versus 91% for larger commercial APIs on Amul's field evaluation sets. Model capability is not the binding constraint.
- **CEO conversion required seeing his own data.** 🟡 The Amul CEO moved from "data is the new oil, I don't understand what that means" to "after I saw data coming together, I started seeing the magic" — not through a presentation but through experiential proof on his own operations. A slide deck could not have produced that conversion.
- **Plus-one scaling pattern documented.** 🟡 Sarlaben advisory → AI-based booking for artificial insemination → microcredit via cooperative bank → Bharat Taxi via open network protocols. Each step builds on existing readiness. Never plus-ten.
- **Agency test: positive outcome documented.** 🟡 Younger family members without 30 years of dairy experience can now access expert knowledge through Sarlaben: "informed, therefore powerful to make choices; freedom comes from knowing, and nobody can fool me."
- **Agency test: risk also named.** 🟡 "There is skill in the hand that counts for more than information in the head." AI should make the farmer's skill more precise, not replace it. If the pathway produces people who cannot function when the AI fails, the system works until it doesn't.
- **Cooperative as network operator.** 🟡 Amul itself plays the D4 role — it says "this runs in my name" and has the institutional standing to back that claim within the cooperative network.
- **Voice-first for excluded producers.** 🟡 Women dairy producers who have never had a smartphone, who may not read with ease — reach Sarlaben by calling 08035453545 in Gujarati. The system draws from their specific animal's records and returns personalised guidance. "The questions they are asking are the same questions they have always had. What has changed is that there is now a system that can answer them."
- **Expansion planned.** 🟡 Planned expansion to 20 Indian languages via Bhashini, reaching 20,000+ villages across 20 states.
