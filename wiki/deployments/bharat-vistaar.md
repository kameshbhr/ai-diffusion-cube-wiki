**Organisation:** Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India 🟡
**Sector:** Agriculture (national coordination layer)

**Period:** Announced in Union Budget 2026-27; formally launched 17 February 2026, Jaipur 🟡

## One-paragraph summary

Bharat-VISTAAR is India's national DPI for agriculture — what UPI is for payments, extended to agricultural information and services. Formally launched by Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan in Jaipur on 17 February 2026 and championed by Prime Minister Modi at the India AI Impact Summit, it provides shared knowledge base, scheme connectivity, AgriStack integration, and ICAR integration that individual state and cooperative deployments (nodes) inherit rather than build independently. Its Rs. 150 crore allocation creates infrastructure that amortises costs across all nodes. The model demonstrates hub-and-spoke federation in practice: each node (MahaVISTAAR for Maharashtra, Amul AI for Gujarat, Bihar Krishi for Bihar) retains local specificity while drawing on shared national infrastructure. Early evidence from the Saagu Baagu pilot in Telangana — on which the design drew — showed a 21% increase in yield per acre and 9% reduction in pesticide use for cotton farmers. 🟡

## Key facts for adopters

- **Rs. 150 crore national allocation amortises infrastructure costs across all nodes.** 🟡 State deployments do not bear the full cost of knowledge base, AgriStack, or ICAR integration.
- **Each node retains local specificity.** 🟡 MahaVISTAAR (Maharashtra), Amul AI (Gujarat), Bihar Krishi (Bihar) each maintained their own deployment context while connecting to the national shared layer.
- **National ministry as network operator.** 🟡 The D4 role is played by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare — an entity with standing to say "this runs in my name" at national scale.
- **Hub-and-spoke enables federation without requiring full consensus.** 🟡 Nodes share knowledge base and protocol substrate (Beckn) without needing to agree on data formats or governance structures before connecting.
- **Phase-1 content at launch.** 🟡 10 major central schemes (PM-KISAN, PMFBY, Soil Health Card, Kisan Credit Card); ICAR crop and livestock guidance; real-time IMD weather and NPSS pest alerts. Feature phone access via 155261.
- **Pilot evidence from Saagu Baagu (Telangana) informed design.** 🟡 21% increase in yield per acre; 9% reduction in pesticide use for cotton farmers. This is not Bharat-VISTAAR evidence directly — it is the evidence base on which the national design drew.
- **PM Modi championed at India AI Impact Summit.** 🟡 Political endorsement at the highest level — documented as a trust and awareness signal for state-level adoption.
