# Livelihoods — Pathway Index

Livelihoods deployments cover AI applications that address income generation, skills development, and economic resilience — including urban and peri-urban livelihoods, vocational training, financial inclusion, MSME advisory, and employment placement. The AI Diffusion Cube does not yet have documented livelihoods-specific pathways in its current intake; the three source documents ingested to date are all agricultural advisory deployments. This sector page is a placeholder for future contributions.

Common challenges in livelihoods AI deployments — from prior field reports outside this wiki — include: user populations with varied occupational backgrounds (unlike agriculture, where the use case is relatively uniform); language and literacy diversity in urban informal employment contexts; data scarcity about informal labour markets; and the need to connect AI outputs to tangible opportunities (job matching, loan applications, scheme eligibility) rather than just advisory.

## Pathways in this sector

No livelihoods pathways are currently documented. If you have experience deploying AI for livelihoods outcomes — urban employment, MSME advisory, skilling, financial inclusion — see the [framework page](../framework.md) for the contribution methodology.
