# Amul

**Type:** Organisation **Role:** Deployer (cooperative) **Geography:** India — Gujarat, 18,500+ villages; expansion planned to 20+ states **Contact or reference:** amul.com

## What they are

Amul (Gujarat Cooperative Milk Marketing Federation) is India's largest dairy cooperative, serving 3.6 million milk producers — predominantly women — across 18,500+ villages in Gujarat. Amul's relevance to AI diffusion is threefold. First, it is the deployer of Amul Sarlaben — the fastest documented deployment on the OAN pathway (three weeks from commitment to full cooperative scale). Second, Amul's 50-year cooperative data foundation (2 billion milk procurement transactions, records on 30 million cattle with unique IDs, 1,200+ veterinary doctors' records annually) is the richest data foundation of any documented deployment in this wiki, and the primary reason the three-week deployment was possible. Third, the Amul deployment was referenced by Prime Minister Modi at the India AI Impact Summit 2026 as a proof of AI advisory at cooperative scale.

Amul is a private cooperative — not a government department. Its approval process (cooperative leadership decision, not government procurement) and its member relationship (daily milk procurement with 3.6 million members) make its deployment dynamics different from government deployments. The cooperative context is relevant for any deployer considering AI deployment within a membership-based institution.

## When this entity is relevant to an adopter

An adopter deploying AI within a cooperative, member-based, or vertically integrated institution — particularly one with an existing member data foundation. Amul's cooperative architecture (self-contained data, agile approval, daily member touchpoints) enabled a deployment timeline impossible in most government contexts. The cooperative data architecture pattern and the plus-one service extension sequence are the primary transferable insights for this adopter type.
